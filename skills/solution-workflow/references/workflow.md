# Solution project lifecycle

## 1. Presales

Record the initial contact, customer alias, business objective, current process, affected users, site or system boundary, budget signal, target schedule, decision process, and known constraints.

Prepare a presales pack register rather than a single generic proposal. The register normally covers product introduction, preliminary solution, meeting agenda, demonstration material, trial boundary, and questions requiring confirmation.

After the formal meeting, produce two separate records:

- External discovery record: confirmed customer requirements, assumptions, boundary, budget and schedule signals, and unanswered questions.
- Internal action list: task, owner, due date, dependency, expected evidence, and escalation condition.

Start a POC only when its objective, data or objects, environment, integration dependency, success criteria, failure scripts, fallback, owner, and sign-off are explicit.

## 2. Tender

Treat the issued tender and amendments as a new baseline. Do not assume they match the discovery record.

Extract instructions, qualification conditions, mandatory clauses, scoring criteria, technical requirements, commercial handoffs, schedule, deliverables, acceptance, certificates, drawings, and disqualification risks. Every row needs a source locator.

Build the technical volume from reviewed requirements and current company materials. Maintain the response matrix throughout drafting and after every amendment. A response is not complete because prose exists; it is complete only when the requirement, evidence, owner, formal response, deviation, section, and review state agree.

## 3. Award and handover

Build the handover baseline from the award letter, final clarifications, contract technical attachment, and generated final bid files. Keep temporary post-award changes separate and apply them before conflicting award or bid content. Record the conflict and the source used.

List the actual participating departments before splitting work. For each department, record its responsibility boundary, owner, default deliverable type, and default response method. Then split each interface, drawing, BOM, deployment task, software package, test, training item, certificate, unresolved point, and change into an independently verifiable task.

Every task needs a department, owner, due date, dependency, expected deliverable, response method, source basis, and acceptance criteria. A department may respond with a file, a written report, a software package or controlled path, a status confirmation, or a combination. Do not force a software build into a document upload, and do not accept a status-only reply when the task requires a drawing, BOM, test record, or other file evidence.

## Gate definitions

- Discovery gate: customer objective and boundary are documented.
- POC gate: success, failure, fallback, and sign-off are documented.
- Requirement gate: tender rows have source locators and review states.
- Draft gate: technical sections map to requirements and evidence.
- Submission gate: approved wording, deviations, attachments, and final checks are complete.
- Handover gate: the awarded baseline and changes are reconciled, every task has an owner and acceptance criteria, and each submitted or accepted task has the required response record.
